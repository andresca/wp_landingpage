Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Who we are

websportal.dev is a web development studio. Questions about this policy: ankbape@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, email, company (optional) and message. We don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

How it's used

Your message is delivered to our inbox through the Gmail API so we can reply to you. It isn't sold, shared for marketing, or added to any mailing list. The site only uses the Gmail permission to send these notifications to us.

Services involved

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, and messages are sent through Google (Gmail). Both process data under their own privacy policies. The "Buy me a coffee" button is loaded from buymeacoffee.com.

Your browser

Your language preference is saved in your browser's local storage. No cookies are set by this site.

Retention and your rights

Messages stay in our email until they're no longer needed. You can ask us to see or delete anything you've sent by writing to ankbape@gmail.com.
