FAQ

How do you price projects?

Usually a fixed price for an agreed scope, set after discovery. For ongoing work I also offer a monthly retainer.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope: a small internal tool can take a few weeks, a full platform a few months. You'll see it in the proposal.

Who owns the code?

You do. At the end of the project you get the code, the documentation and access to the infrastructure.

Do you offer maintenance?

Yes: updates, monitoring, bug fixes and new features, as you need them.
